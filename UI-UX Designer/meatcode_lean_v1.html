What drives roasted, meaty notes in plant-based beef?▋
Precursors for 2-acetyl-1-pyrroline Reduce beany off-notes in pea protein Maillard vs lipid oxidation in analogs

Answer

Roasted, meaty character in plant-based beef comes mainly from Maillard chemistry during cooking — reducing sugars reacting with amino acids and peptides to form nitrogen- and sulfur-bearing volatiles (pyrazines, thiazoles). Lipid oxidation contributes fatty and some meaty notes, and the two pathways interact.

Because analogs are lower in the right precursors than muscle, added cysteine–ribose systems and thiamine are common routes to boost the roasted profile.

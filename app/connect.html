Use your own Claude

Link your Claude organization and the Oracle questions you ask are billed to it, under a monthly limit you set.

Linking Claude isn't switched on for this server yet.

You'll go to Claude to approve, then come straight back here. Anthropic's linked billing is a beta feature and may change.

What happens next

  • You sign in to Claude and pick the organization to link.
  • You set a monthly spend limit on Claude's own page.
  • You land back in MeatCODE, ready to ask the Oracle.
You'll need the Admin or Billing role in a Claude organization that pays Anthropic directly (API credits or an invoice). A Claude chat subscription on its own isn't enough. No organization yet? Create one at platform.claude.com.

You stay in control

  • MeatCODE bills only the Oracle requests you make, and never receives your Claude password.
  • Unlink any time here or in the Claude Console; billing stops immediately.
  • Good to know: these requests run in MeatCODE's Claude organization, so its data-retention terms apply rather than yours.

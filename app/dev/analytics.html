Analytics Zone

Analytical methods for flavor work

The five measurement families MeatCODE plans to speak fluently. Status chips are honest: only GC-MS has reference data in the database today.

GC-MSgas chromatography–mass spectrometry

DATA EXISTS

Separates and identifies the volatile compounds that make up aroma — the workhorse for profiling meaty top-notes.

In the DB: the Meaty Volatile Library — reference volatiles with retention indices, usable as clearly-labelled reference data (GC-MS journey itself is preview-scope).

HPLChigh-performance liquid chromatography

PLANNED

Quantifies non-volatile taste and precursor molecules — amino acids, sugars, nucleotides — the inputs that cooking turns into flavor.

OlfactoryGC-O & sensory panels

PLANNED

Puts human noses on the data — which detected compounds actually smell, how intense, and what descriptor they evoke.

H/C-NMR¹H / ¹³C nuclear magnetic resonance

PLANNED

Resolves molecular structure and tracks reaction chemistry — confirming what a Maillard system actually produced.

SpectroscopyUV-Vis / IR / fluorescence

PLANNED

Fast optical readouts of browning, oxidation and pigment state — cheap process-level signals to complement the deep methods.

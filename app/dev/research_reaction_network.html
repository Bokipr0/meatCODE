Research · how meaty flavor forms
Where does the flavor start?
Meaty flavor is built during cooking. Pick which family of precursors you want to explore — MeatCODE walks you through the heat-driven reaction network it feeds, the volatile aroma compounds it produces, and how those read on analytical and sensory data.

Lipid precursors

Fats, fatty acids and triglycerides. Under heat these oxidise — hydroperoxides break down into the fatty, green and fried-note aldehydes and furans.

fats / fatty acids → lipid oxidation → volatiles
Explore the lipid network →

Aqueous precursors

Peptides, sugars and amino acids. Under heat these drive the Maillard reaction and Strecker degradation — the roasted, meaty, sulfurous thiols and pyrazines.

amino acids + sugars → Maillard + Strecker → volatiles
Explore the aqueous network →

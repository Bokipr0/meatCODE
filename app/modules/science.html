Where does meaty flavor come from?

Follow one piece of meat from raw to cooked. Tap any label to dig deeper.

FatLipids that break down under heat. Much of each species' own smell starts here. JuiceThe water phase: sugars, amino acids, peptides, nucleotides and vitamins. Meat matrixFibres, connective tissue and water. They shape heat, contact and release. ProcessingTime, temperature, moisture, pH and cooking method. Conditions vary; none are fixed. Aroma · 3 familiesVolatile molecules you smell: Maillard & Strecker, lipid oxidation and sulfur chemistry. TasteNon-volatiles on the tongue: umami, salt, sweet, bitter. ReleaseHow the matrix lets aroma and taste out as you chew.

Bring your own Claude

Oracle questions can run on your own Claude organization, so the usage is billed to you and under your control — you choose a monthly spend limit when you connect, and you can disconnect at any time.

  • Create a MeatCODE account, then choose Connect your Claude organization on your account page.
  • You approve on Claude's own page — MeatCODE never sees your Claude password.
  • You need the Admin or Billing role in a Claude organization that pays Anthropic directly (API credits or invoice). A Claude chat subscription on its own isn't enough.
Claude linked billing is a beta feature from Anthropic and may change.
